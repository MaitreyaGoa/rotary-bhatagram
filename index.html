Why Bhatagram?

Bhatagrama is an ancient name recorded for Sankhali, in our own Bicholim taluka.

When our founding members looked for a name, we wanted one that belongs to this land. Bhatagram carries our taluka's history, and it gives our club an identity of its own alongside the Rotary Club of Bicholim, which has served the town since 1993.

We are lawyers, doctors, chartered accountants, teachers and business owners. Rotary membership is by invitation, and we started this club to bring a new generation into service for Bicholim and the villages around it.
